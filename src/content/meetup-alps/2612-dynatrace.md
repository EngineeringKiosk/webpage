    title: 'TinyML on a Bike'
    description: |
      What does it take to run a neural network on a microcontroller with 320 KB of RAM? Conventional AI devours memory, energy and compute, which are exactly the things a microcontroller has very little of. In this talk, we look at why TinyML is worth the trouble anyway and how ML models can be made small.

      I will then tell the story of how we used TinyML in a citizen science project: a bike-mounted device that detects when cars overtake dangerously close. We ended up building it twice, with different sensors, hardware and models, so you will see two different paths to the same goal.
